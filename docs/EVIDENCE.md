## 1. The Wellness Inventory (the instrument)
- **Validation paper.** Swarbrick M, Di Bello A, Eissenstat SJ, Nemec PB, Hien DA, Gill KJ. Factor Structure, Reliability, and Construct Validity of the Wellness Inventory. *Psychiatric Services* 2025;76(3):263-269. Published online 5 November 2024. doi:10.1176/appi.ps.20230622. PubMed 39497530. https://www.psychiatryonline.org/doi/full/10.1176/appi.ps.20230622
- **What it validated.** "Factor analyses yielded a one-factor model comprising 54 items." Sample "N=3,446; 50% White and 43% female", three samples (N=1,679; 1,693; 74), "students and faculty members in a public university and behavioral health providers", data collected "between October 5, 2022, and February 10, 2023". Starting instrument: 67 items across eight dimensions. Scale: "(1, never true; 2, rarely true; 3, sometimes true; 4, always true)".
- **Numbers.** CFA "RMSEA=0.031, 90% CI=0.030-0.033; CFI=0.90; TLI=0.89; and SRMR=0.04"; "Cronbach's alpha=0.95"; test-retest at one month "r=0.67, p<0.001"; convergent validity with the Perceived Wellness Survey "r=0.61" and the Engagement in Meaningful Activities Survey "r=0.76"; DASS-21 "r=-0.21".
- **Dimensions.** The paper found a single factor: "The findings support a single dimension of wellness, suggesting wellness as a holistic, general construct." Table 2 groups the 54 items under the eight dimension headings (physical 7, intellectual 4, environmental 6, spiritual 4, emotional 9, financial 6, social 6, occupational 12). Our per-dimension feedback uses those headings as the student-facing frame, not as validated subscales. Say that if asked.
- **No 18-item short form exists in the paper or on any Rutgers or CSPNJ page we found.** Rutgers describes the ScarletWell inventory as "The 54-item checklist" (https://www.rutgers.edu/news/wellness-inventory-rutgers-researchers-receives-empirically-valid-gold-star, 2025-01-08) and "a 54-question self-assessment" (https://newbrunswick.rutgers.edu/news/how-scarletwell-redefining-personal-wellness-rutgers-and-world, 2024-11-12). Our two-minute quick set is a team choice and is labelled as such in the app.
- **Permission.** "Do not adapt, modify, or use the wellness inventory without the explicit permission of the Dr. Peggy Swarbrick." https://alcoholstudies.rutgers.edu/wellness-in-recovery/inventory/ . Ask her (she is a mentor) before any pilot.
- **Model paper.** Swarbrick M. A wellness approach. *Psychiatric Rehabilitation Journal* 2006;29(4):311-314. doi:10.2975/29.2006.311.314

## 2. ScarletWell
- Tagline: "A public health and prevention-focused approach to mental health and wellness for Rutgers-New Brunswick's students, faculty, and staff." https://newbrunswick.rutgers.edu/scarletwell
- "Scarlet Well is a novel public health and prevention-focused program promoting the holistic well-being of Rutgers-New Brunswick's 79,000+ students, faculty, and staff as part of the university's Academic Master Plan." Inaugural year 2024-25. Director Peggy Swarbrick, PhD. https://newbrunswick.rutgers.edu/scarletwell/about
- By the numbers (same page): "65 Peer Support Leader Digital Badges Awarded"; "42 Wellness at Work and Connection Grants Funded"; "2,300 Participants in Grant Funded Activities"; "34 Scarlet Well Wellness & Self-Care Trainings/Workshops"; "1,622 Participants Reached with Trainings".
- Grants: "Wellness Connection Grants (for student-led/faculty sponsored) and Wellness at Work Grants (for faculty/staff-led)", 2026-27 awards "ranging from $200-$ 1,000", applications were due May 15, 2026. https://newbrunswick.rutgers.edu/scarletwell/about and https://facultysuccess.rutgers.edu/event/scarletwell-2026-2027-wellness-grants-call-for-proposals/
- **The MyRutgers widget.** Live since September 2025 with the Wellness Calendar and the resource list (ScarletWell LinkedIn, 2025-09-22). Its own paper describes "the Events tab and the Check-in tab" and lists "wellness Inventory self-assessment, chatbot integration, and personalized data analytics" as **future directions**: Ramachandran S, Nguyen J, Spagnolo A, Swarbrick M, Janelli R, Haghani S. Engineering a Culture of Wellness: Design and Deployment of a University Wellness Widget. ComManTel 2025, pp. 179-182. doi:10.1109/ComManTel68363.2025.11368432. **Pitch line: we built their stated next step.** Do not claim the widget already runs the inventory.
