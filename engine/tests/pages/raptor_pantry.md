We are now proud to serve over 200 students per week. The food pantry is open Tuesday and Thursday from 1pm- 4pm and is open to all full-time and part-time students.

## Hours of Operation

The Scarlet Raptor's Nest is open for the semester, and students are welcome to visit once a week.

**Online Orders**: Place your order between **10:00 AM – 1:00 PM** on **Monday** and pick it up on **Tuesday** between **12:00 PM – 3:00 PM.**
**In-Person Shopping** (**Wednesdays**): Schedule your appointment through **PantrySoft** and shop in person from **12:00 PM – 3:45 PM**.

**Please note**: We are **no** longer open on **Thursdays** for pantry distribution.
